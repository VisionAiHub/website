# A new home for VisionAiHub

We've rebuilt our website on a modern, code-driven stack — Next.js, Tailwind, and MDX —
so we can ship updates faster, write better content, and serve our readers more performant pages.

## Why we moved

The old site got us off the ground, but it became a bottleneck whenever we wanted to:

- Add structured pages for new services or industries
- Publish technical articles with code samples
- Tune SEO and performance precisely
- Integrate analytics, A/B testing, and CRM hooks cleanly

## What's next

Expect more in-depth writing on Generative AI, agents, and applied ML —
plus case studies from our delivery work across Germany.
